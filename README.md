## How to use Luna

- Tap the number and operation keys to enter a calculation.
- Press `=` to see the answer.
- Use `AC` to clear the expression, or the delete key to remove the last character.
- Use `%`, parentheses, and `±` for more options.
- Open **History** to revisit recent calculations.
- Open **Settings** to choose a theme, adjust text and key sizes, and toggle animations or key sounds.

Your calculation history and preferences are stored on your device.
